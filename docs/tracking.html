Post-Launch Tracking
Salesforce + Anthropic — Claudeforce  sustained
Week 12 · first profiled 2026-08-28 · sustained-GTM 6/10 (launch moment 8.5)

Anthropic's own properties now carry what Salesforce's page still won't — the first named Claudeforce customers (GitLab, Siemens, Legora) and a 7,000-seller usage number — while Salesforce announced Copilotforce with Microsoft on Nov 17, putting the same "Salesforce inside someone else's assistant" motion inside Copilot and quietly draining the exclusivity out of "the #1 AI meets the #1 CRM."

Shipped vs promisedThe core promise — open beta in September 2026 — was kept on Sept 15 and is now corroborated by at least six independent partner write-ups. Twelve weeks later salesforce.com/claudeforce still has not said so. The hero subhead is byte-identical across all three snapshots: "available today for pilot customers and is planned for open beta in September 2026," and "Salesforce in Claude — available today for select pilot customers" is still the first line of available_now. That is now a roughly ten-week-stale description of the flagship product's own lifecycle stage on the flagship page. Genuine shipping since tracking start: AIforce (MCP servers, APIs, CLI tools) moved into available_now, as did the Salesforce MCP server with discover/describe/dispatch and read-only dispatch, Agentforce Voice, Piper, and overlapping-territory support in sales planning. Enterprise Frontier Safeguards remains at "rolling out in phases beginning later fall/autumn 2026" — due within weeks, and the single most checkable commitment left before year end. Pricing is the first real movement in 12 weeks, and it is half a move: the pricing array is now populated with two entries (Claude billed inside Agentforce or purchased separately and connected through Salesforce and Spiff MCP servers; Spiff MCP included with the Spiff license) and one entry — "Salesforce in Claude" — whose price field is literally an empty string. Off-page, the numbers that buyers actually asked for are starting to surface from other sources: Agentforce Coworker at $50/user/month from Nov 4 (reflektlab), a separate capped Claude line item reported Oct 23 that moved SF shares 3.5% (fiduciarytech), and Flex Credit metering on agentic MCP and API calls still unpriced (Synechron). The thinned coming_soon list (16 items at tracking start, 9 now) should still be read as an extraction artifact, not cancellations: eesel's Nov 18 review of the live page reports Service, Marketing, Commerce, Field Service "and the rest" are all still labelled COMING SOON, and the Marketing entry is still truncated mid-sentence in the snapshot. One quiet removal that is probably real: events_mentioned collapsed from seven (Dreamforce, TDX, Connections, Tableau Conference, Informatica World, Agentforce World Tours) to effectively one webinar.
Channel activityThe GTM motion has effectively relocated to Anthropic and to the partner channel. The highest-value asset of this checkpoint is Anthropic's IPO disclosure package: ipo.anthropic.com publishes a formal Salesforce case study with audited-grade numbers — +151% effective output per developer, a 33-endpoint API migration done in 13 days against a 231-person-day estimate, 8.1M annualized hours from Slackbot, 99% of Salesforce employees using Slackbot, 60%+ of 83,000 employees using Claude. Anthropic's own "Bringing Salesforce into Claude" post is now cited (eesel, Nov 18) as naming GitLab, Siemens and Legora as deployments and 7,000 Salesforce sellers in production. None of that appears on the Claudeforce page. The webinar "How Anthropic Runs Sales Performance with Claudeforce" is still the only event on the page, but it now has a named speaker roster in the snapshot notes (Kevin Jordan of Anthropic, Andy Kung, Catie Shadley, Chase Evans, Amin Bouabdellah), which suggests it actually ran and is available on demand rather than sitting as a placeholder. The SI channel is the clearest growth signal: AllCloud, Synechron, Teqfocus, 360degreecloud, CWT Studio, Nespon, Cloud Odyssey, Condado, Breadwinner and Catalyst (Australia) have all published Claudeforce explainers, readiness assessments or dedicated service pages between Oct 20 and Nov 18. CWT Studio has built a "Claudeforce readiness" CRM scoring tool with a booking calendar; Nespon has a standing /salesforce/claudeforce and /salesforce/aiforce practice page; Sirocco publishes a Flex Credit calculator. Teqfocus describes itself as a "Salesforce Summit Partner and Anthropic Select Partner," and Catalyst announced joining the Anthropic Partner Network — so Anthropic has stood up partner tiering around this, which is real channel infrastructure rather than press-release noise. Teqfocus also surfaces a number Salesforce has not promoted: a $300M commitment in Anthropic tokens for 2026. What I could not find: any Salesforce-run Claudeforce customer webinar series, any TDX or World Tour session content specific to Claudeforce after Dreamforce, any paid or field campaign evidence, or an install-the-plugin path on the Salesforce page. Those may exist and not be retrievable; I am reporting the gap, not asserting absence.
Third-party validationMaterially stronger than at week 8, and almost all of it is third-party by origin. Named customers finally exist — GitLab, Siemens and Legora, per Anthropic's blog as reported by eesel on Nov 18 — which closes the single biggest hole flagged at weeks 4 and 8. Usage: 7,000 Salesforce sellers on the plugin. Anthropic's IPO case study is the strongest artifact of the whole campaign because it is a disclosure document, not marketing: +151% developer output, 18x faster API migration, 99% Slackbot adoption, with a footnoted caveat that Salesforce beneficially owns under 1% of Anthropic. Salesforce Ben quantifies the financial entanglement from the other side: a ~$5.1B Anthropic stake, $2.7B in unrealized gains in H1 FY27, about 45% of Salesforce's $11.3B strategic investment portfolio. Synechron's Principal Technical Architect published the most useful buyer-side note — "Claudeforce has moved from a keynote demo to a working beta that teams use," paired with a warning that Salesforce's own Koa model will likely take routine CRM work, that customers pay two separate bills, and that the Flex Credit price isn't set so budget for it now. That is validation of the product and a flag on the commercial model in the same breath. I found no Gartner, Forrester or IDC note specific to Claudeforce.
Messaging evolutionThree changes worth tracking. First, hard numbers moved from footnotes into positioning: ROI in ~8 months, 53% employee adoption, 29% CSAT improvement, 29% operational cost reduction, and Service Cloud's 38/30/38 now sit in positioning_claims rather than buried in blog copy. A new privacy line was added — "Data never stored or used to train Claude" — layered on top of the existing zero-data-retention claim, which reads like a response to enterprise security review friction. Second, Benioff's philosophical hook — "Probabilistic intelligence alone doesn't run a company; deterministic systems don't reason" — has quietly disappeared from positioning_claims, as has the sharper "no new permissions model" phrasing. The page is getting more statistical and less memorable. Third, and most consequential, the architecture layer has been renamed twice in twelve weeks: Headless 360 became AIforce at Dreamforce and, per AllCloud and Synechron, has since been renamed again to SIforce. The Claudeforce page still says Headless 360 and AIforce. Partners are now writing explainers whose primary job is disambiguating Salesforce's own vocabulary — AllCloud literally calls it "buzzword soup," Breadwinner's top-ranking asset is "Agentforce vs Claudeforce: what is the difference," because buyers think they are competing products. Meanwhile the strategic frame is eroding from outside: Copilotforce with Microsoft (Ignite, Nov 17) puts Salesforce inside Copilot and Teams on the same pattern, Missionforce extends to NVIDIA and OpenAI, and Koa is Salesforce's own in-house CRM reasoning model. Twelve weeks after "the #1 AI meets the #1 CRM," Claude is demonstrably one of several front doors, and the Claudeforce page has not adjusted a word to account for it.
Hiring signalNo new Claudeforce-specific postings surfaced this checkpoint beyond the "Senior Manager, Partner GTM – AIforce" role found at week 8, which named Claudeforce a flagship AI partnership. Retrieval here is thin and I would not read that as a freeze. The stronger proxy is channel staffing on the other side of the table: Teqfocus now carries both Salesforce Summit Partner and Anthropic Select Partner status, Catalyst announced joining the Anthropic Partner Network, and at least ten SIs have published standing Claudeforce practice pages, readiness assessments or billing calculators — none of which get built without someone being paid to staff them. Anthropic formalizing partner tiers is itself the hiring signal that matters, because it means the motion is being resourced as a channel program rather than a co-marketing moment.
Steal This — Sustained GTMDo the unglamorous work of retiring your own launch copy. Salesforce kept every promise that mattered — beta on time, real logos, 7,000 sellers, an IPO-grade case study — and then let the one page carrying the brand name sit untouched for twelve weeks, still telling visitors the beta is "planned for September 2026" and still routing the only CTA to Anthropic's announcement. The consequence is not cosmetic: when your own page is stale, the ecosystem writes your documentation for you. The best Claudeforce explainers in market right now belong to eesel, AllCloud, Synechron and a dozen SIs, and they are the ones ranking, the ones capturing the "what does it cost" query, and the ones attaching their own CTA to it. Partner content is leverage only if your canonical page is the thing it points back to. Build a post-launch page owner with a weekly obligation — stage label, pricing field, install path, newest named customer — and treat "a commitment shipped but the page doesn't say so" as a P1 bug, not a backlog item. The second lesson is about exclusivity framing: if you lead with "the #1 AI meets the #1 CRM" and then ship the identical motion with Microsoft eleven weeks later, you need a prepared answer about why the first partnership is still different. Salesforce doesn't have one on the page, so Synechron wrote the "two-model bet, budget for both bills" frame instead, and that is now the narrative buyers meet.
Why this sustained score: The 8.5 launch score reflected an unusually complete announcement: two CEOs on stage, a named product, 37 enumerated skills, a dated beta commitment, a governance story, and a clear CTA. Twelve weeks on, the underlying business has delivered more than the launch promised — beta on time, GitLab/Siemens/Legora deployed, 7,000 sellers, $300M in token commitment, a dozen-plus SI practices, an Anthropic IPO case study with disclosure-grade numbers, and the beginnings of real pricing. By substance, this is one of the better-executed partnerships of the year. The gap to 6.0 is almost entirely a campaign-execution gap, and it is concentrated in one place: the owned surface never moved. The hero subhead is byte-identical across three snapshots and is now ten weeks out of date about its own product's availability stage. There is still no install path on the vendor's own page; the only conversion route hands the visitor to Anthropic. The pricing array now exists but the Salesforce in Claude price is an empty string, while partners publish Flex Credit calculators to fill the void. Named customers exist but live on Anthropic's blog, not on salesforce.com/claudeforce, which still lists only Salesforce and Anthropic themselves — a fixable, high-value omission left unfixed for three months. The architecture layer has been renamed twice without the page catching up, so the most-read Claudeforce content in market is partner-written disambiguation. And the strategic frame has been undercut by Salesforce's own subsequent announcements — Copilotforce on Nov 17, Missionforce with OpenAI and NVIDIA, Koa internally — with no narrative adjustment. Momentum is sustained, but it is being sustained by Anthropic and the channel. Salesforce launched brilliantly and then stopped marketing on the asset it built to do the marketing.
