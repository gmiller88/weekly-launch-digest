Post-Launch Tracking
Salesforce + Anthropic — Claudeforce  sustained
Week 4 · first profiled 2026-08-28

Salesforce shipped the Salesforce in Claude open beta on schedule at Dreamforce (Sept 15) and folded Claudeforce into a bigger "AIforce" umbrella with Dario Amodei on the main keynote stage — but the Claudeforce page itself still reads as if the product is in pilot with a beta only "planned," and there is still no pricing and no third-party named customer.

Shipped vs promisedThe single biggest promise — "open beta in September 2026" — appears to have been kept. Third-party coverage (Coursiv, updated Sept 15) reports the open beta went live at Dreamforce and that Salesforce in Claude is available on all paid Claude plans. That is a shipped commitment, and it landed inside the promised month. The problem is that salesforce.com/claudeforce does not say so. Both snapshots carry the identical hero subhead: "available today for pilot customers and is planned for open beta in September 2026," and "Salesforce in Claude — available today for select pilot customers" is still the top line under available_now. The primary CTA did change — from "Get updates (join waitlist for Salesforce in Claude beta)" to a split "read Anthropic's announcement / request access to Enterprise Frontier Safeguards / connect with Salesforce" — which is consistent with retiring the waitlist once beta opened, but it replaced a single clear conversion path with three vaguer ones and no "install the plugin" route. Pricing is still an empty array in both snapshots, four weeks after a LinkedIn commenter on Salesforce's own launch post asked the two questions every buyer has: what is the cost per user, and do you need Agentforce licenses. Coursiv reports the answer is genuinely messy — Salesforce bills headless API consumption, Claude inference is contracted separately with Anthropic, no combined contract — which explains the silence but does not excuse it. The coming_soon list shrank sharply: Commerce, Revenue, Field Service, Tableau, MuleSoft, Informatica, Data 360, Industries all disappeared without reappearing in available_now. Headless 360 did move cleanly from coming_soon into available_now, and Agentforce Voice was added to available_now. I would not over-read the vanished roadmap tiles as cancellations — the Marketing skills entry in the current snapshot is visibly truncated mid-sentence ("Ground every campaign with..."), which suggests the roadmap section is a carousel or accordion that the extractor only partially captured. But the net effect for a visitor is a shorter, vaguer forward roadmap than existed at baseline. Enterprise Frontier Safeguards is unchanged at "phased rollout beginning later fall 2026" — not yet due, not yet slipped.
Channel activityDreamforce 2026 (Sept 15–17, San Francisco) was the entire post-launch motion, and it was substantial. Amodei appeared on the main keynote stage alongside Benioff, plus the CEOs of NVIDIA and Siemens; Benioff called the combined effort "the most exciting thing we've ever done" and described Anthropic as "the number one AI in the world" (R&D World, Sept 15). Salesforce used the event to launch AIforce as the umbrella layer, with Claudeforce positioned as one surface on it alongside Slackforce, Agentforce Coworker and Koa. Salesforce News published a Dreamforce roundup on X ("Everything from Dreamforce 2026: Claudeforce, Koa, and..."). Stack Intelligence reported that Salesforce ran live Claudeforce experiences and workshops on the show floor. Owned social was active across LinkedIn (a four-point "better together" post from the main Salesforce handle), X, and multiple Instagram Reels explaining the 37 skills. A webinar titled "How Anthropic Runs Sales Performance with Claudeforce" is listed on the page in both snapshots — I could not confirm whether it has actually run or whether a recording exists, and it is the single highest-value asset on that list, since it is the only thing that would turn the "Anthropic is our customer" claim into a watchable proof point. Partner and SI content filled fast: Cybage, Codleo, CodeSierra, Stack Intelligence and Trigger Hours all published explainers within days, and a PwC-linked LinkedIn post references a webinar series built around ClaudeForce. That ecosystem content is real distribution, but it is uncontrolled — CodeSierra's guide, which ranks, asserts the default reasoning model is "Claude 3.5 Sonnet," which is wrong. When you don't publish your own technical FAQ fast enough, partners will write it for you and get it wrong.
Third-party validationStrongest proof is financial, not customer. The announcement ran on the Q2 FY27 earnings call; CRM closed up roughly 22.6% at $252.05 the next day, one of the strongest sessions in company history (cmm360). Agentforce + Data 360 combined ARR hit ~$3.9B, up more than 210%, with 3.2B agentic work units executed across Agentforce and Slack, up 97% QoQ. Benioff added a usable internal proof point at Dreamforce: the Hunter sales agent generated $500M in pipeline last quarter. Ecosystem coverage has been continuous rather than a single spike — Salesforce Ben has run Claudeforce as a recurring beat, including the sceptical "Is Agentforce Still Relevant Now That Salesforce Has Claudeforce?" and "What Does the Future Hold for Anthropic and Salesforce?" Cybage's analysis is the sharpest and least flattering: it reads the press release as Salesforce "accepting a future where they become a headless system of workflows and records," and notes the awkwardness of a CRM vendor conceding that "where sellers work" is moving to Anthropic. Counterweights are visible: a circulating Gartner prediction that 70% of enterprises will abandon agentic AI by 2028, and Salesforce Ben's report of a global service disruption during Dreamforce itself. The material gap is customer validation. named_customers is identical in both snapshots — Salesforce and Anthropic, nobody else. Four weeks after launch and two weeks after open beta, there is not one third-party enterprise telling its own Claudeforce story that I could find.
Messaging evolutionTwo changes worth noting. First, hard numbers got promoted from buried blog copy into headline positioning: "ROI in about eight months, 53% employee adoption, 29% CSAT improvement, 29% reduction in operational costs" and the Agentforce Service stats (38% CSAT, 30% lower support costs, 38% more cases solved) were in the baseline only as notes, and now sit in positioning_claims. That is the right direction — moving from vision language to quantified outcomes — though the numbers are Agentforce-derived, not Claudeforce-derived, which a sharp buyer will notice. Second, the claims got tighter and slightly less expansive: "Software is a system that powers every interface; the UI is the AI" compressed to just "The UI is the AI"; "Salesforce is Anthropic's preferred CRM; Slack its preferred multiplayer work platform" lost the Slack half; and "works with existing Salesforce permissions and business rules" quietly dropped "and business rules." Modules picked up "MCP servers, APIs, and CLI tools" — the AIforce harness vocabulary from Dreamforce starting to leak onto the Claudeforce page — while Koa dropped out of the module list into related-coverage mentions, i.e. it became its own thing. The bigger repositioning happened off-page: Claudeforce is now a component of AIforce rather than the top-level story. That is strategically coherent but it means the Claudeforce sub-brand, which had enormous earned-media equity three weeks earlier, is now a child node whose landing page has not been rewritten to reflect its new place.
Hiring signalThin and mostly indirect. The one concrete find is Anthropic hiring a Salesforce Developer at $270–345K to build and run its own Salesforce platform for quote-to-cash — core sales automation, CPQ, order and contract management — with the posting explicitly stating the team is "AI-first" and expects Claude to be used to write and ship the code. That is a useful reinforcement of the mutual-adoption claim: Anthropic is genuinely staffing Salesforce as its revenue system of record, not just name-dropping it. I could not find dedicated Claudeforce product marketing, partner marketing, or field enablement roles at either company, and absence of retrieval here is not evidence they don't exist — Salesforce's careers site is large and these roles are rarely titled after a sub-brand.
Steal This — Sustained GTMWhen you launch a sub-brand three weeks before your flagship conference and then absorb it into a bigger umbrella at that conference, someone has to own rewriting the sub-brand's landing page the same week. Salesforce shipped the beta on time — the hardest part — and then left the page saying the beta was "planned." A visitor arriving today from any of the dozens of ecosystem explainers still ranking for "Claudeforce" reads a pilot-stage pitch, loses the waitlist CTA that used to give them something to do, and finds no pricing and no way to install. The portable lesson: the week-one landing page is a launch asset, but by week four it is a demand-capture asset, and it needs different copy — shipped status, a self-serve path, and an answer to the packaging question your own LinkedIn commenters are asking in public. The avoidable failure here is not silence; it is a loud campaign pointing at a page frozen in its announcement-day state.
What would make this strong by week 12: Too early to score, and in this case a four-week read is especially misleading because Dreamforce was always on the calendar — a launch timed three weeks ahead of your own flagship event gets a second wave by design, not by sustained effort. What we can say now: the hardest promise (September open beta) appears kept, executive air cover was maximal, and third-party coverage has been continuous rather than a single news spike. What is missing is exactly the stuff that separates a sustained campaign from a well-amplified announcement. By week 12 I would want to see: (1) at least two non-Salesforce, non-Anthropic enterprises telling their own Claudeforce story with named people and a number attached — the customer-zero framing has carried this for a full quarter and is now overdue for external proof; (2) a real answer on pricing and packaging, even if it is just "here is how headless consumption is metered and here is whether you need Agentforce licenses" — the two questions the audience asked on day one are still unanswered on the page; (3) the Sales-only skill library extended into at least one more persona, since Service, Marketing, Commerce, Revenue and Field Service were all promised "in the next quarter" and several have already quietly thinned out of the roadmap section; (4) the Claudeforce page rewritten to post-beta status with a self-serve install path; (5) evidence the Anthropic sales-performance webinar actually ran and exists as a recorded, gated asset; and (6) Enterprise Frontier Safeguards entering its phased rollout on the stated fall timeline. Hit four of those six and this is an 8-plus at week 12. Keep shipping product while the owned GTM surface stays frozen and it lands closer to a 6, because the launch will have been carried entirely by earned media and an events calendar.
